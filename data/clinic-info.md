# Clínica Sorriso Paulista

Each `## topic` section is one answer for the `get_clinic_info` tool. Keep them short: the agent reads them aloud.

## location
Rua Haddock Lobo, 595, 3rd floor, Cerqueira César, São Paulo. Two blocks from Consolação metro station (green and yellow lines).

## parking
The building has paid valet parking at R$ 25 for up to two hours. There's also a public parking lot on Rua Bela Cintra, half a block away.

## insurance
We accept Amil Dental, OdontoPrev, SulAmérica Odonto and Porto Seguro Odonto. Whitening isn't covered by any plan. Bring your insurance card and an ID to the appointment.

## payment
Pix, debit and credit cards. Treatments over R$ 800 (whitening, root canal) can be split into up to 10 interest-free installments on a credit card. We don't accept checks.

## cancellation_policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Two missed appointments without notice mean future bookings need a deposit by Pix.

## first_visit
The first check-up is free and takes about 30 minutes, including an exam and a treatment plan with prices. Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a health form. X-rays, if needed, are done on site for R$ 80.

## emergencies
For severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth during opening hours, we fit you in the same day: tell the receptionist it's an emergency. Outside opening hours, go to the Hospital Sírio-Libanês emergency room or call SAMU at 192.

## children
We see children from 6 years old, with Dra. Carla Mendes. A parent or guardian must stay during the appointment.

## accessibility
The building has a ramp at the entrance and an elevator, and the clinic has an accessible restroom.

## contact
WhatsApp and phone: (11) 3456-7890. Email: contato@sorrisopaulista.com.br. Staff answer WhatsApp during opening hours.
