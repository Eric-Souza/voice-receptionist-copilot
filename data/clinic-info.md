# Brightside Dental

Each `## topic` section is one answer for the `get_clinic_info` tool. Keep them short: the agent reads them aloud.

## location
1200 South Lamar Boulevard, Suite 300, Austin, Texas. We're on the third floor, above the coffee shop, a short walk from the Barton Springs Road bus stop.

## parking
There's free parking in the garage behind the building, on levels 2 and 3. Street parking on South Lamar is metered.

## insurance
We're in network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and Aetna, and we file claims for most other PPO plans. Whitening isn't covered by any plan. Bring your insurance card and a photo ID to your appointment.

## payment
We take all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, and Apple Pay. Treatments over $500, like whitening or a root canal, can be paid over time with CareCredit. We don't take checks.

## cancellation_policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. After two missed appointments without notice, future bookings need a $50 deposit.

## first_visit
The first check-up is free for new patients and takes about 30 minutes, with an exam and a treatment plan with prices. Please arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a health form. X-rays, if needed, are done here for $75.

## emergencies
For severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth during opening hours, we'll see you the same day: tell the receptionist it's an emergency. Outside opening hours, go to the nearest emergency room, or call 911 if it's life-threatening.

## children
We see children from 6 years old, with Dr. Carla Mendez. A parent or guardian must stay during the appointment.

## accessibility
The building has a step-free entrance and an elevator, and the clinic has an accessible restroom.
